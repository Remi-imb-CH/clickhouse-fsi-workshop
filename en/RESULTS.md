# Results · ClickHouse financial services workshop

The data is simulated and deterministic: **volumes, row counts and business results are identical for every participant**,
whatever the service. They are listed below, checked on a full run of modules 00 to 05 and 07
(module 06 was checked at 1% of its size).

**Durations, memory and compression depend on the service.** The `Measured` column is left empty on purpose:
fill it in after a run on your reference service (the recommended one is 3 replicas of 64 GB, 16 vCPU each),
from the console or from `logs/` after `./run_all.sh`.

> Orders of magnitude to remember
> - **8.9 million** transactions received as **1,860 JSON files** (2.6 GiB), unfolded by 4 materialized views
> - **99.33%** of card-days reconciled to the cent; every break is located to the card and the day
> - A sum in an incremental materialized view is **5.1% too high** after one resent file; `uniqExact` stays exact
> - **1.5 billion** transactions in module 06: one card's statement reads a few thousand rows out of 1.5 billion
